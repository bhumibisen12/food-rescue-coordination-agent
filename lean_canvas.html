Problem 1

  • Perishable Danger Zone: Hot cooked meals spoil in 3–4 hours at >30°C without active thermal/cold-chain routing.
  • Sub-optimal Single Trips: 1-pickup/1-dropoff trips waste volunteer fuel and increase food spoilage risk.
  • Coordinator Burnout: Non-profit coordinators spend 3–5 hours daily on chaotic calls, WhatsApp & Excel sheets.
  • Fraud & Bot Spoofing: Fake bulk donation reports or repeated cancellations waste physical volunteer dispatch.
